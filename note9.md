# note 9 (jewel update 4)

**Jewel Updates — a separate RoTU sighting**

Jewel on the Horizon has been seen again.

The report came from the RoTU member who did not join the latest expedition.
They were sick and stayed behind at headquarters while the rest of the team
went out.

On the way back from the coast, they saw a lighthouse across a lake several
miles from the site. They recognized the shape from the old plans and believe
it was Jewel on the Horizon, not Jewel 2. They watched it for less than a
minute. When they looked again, it was gone.

This was not part of the expedition, and the witness was nowhere near the
other RoTU members when they saw it. Their account is being kept separate
from the team's report.

This is the second recent sighting of what may be Jewel on the Horizon. A
NARAO member previously reported seeing it across the water from the north
shore. The locations are different, but in both accounts the lighthouse
appeared briefly and then disappeared.

We have checked the map. There is no lighthouse marked at the lake, and no
known structure there that matches the description. We do not know how Jewel
on the Horizon could have appeared so far from its original site, or whether
the two sightings have the same explanation.

The RoTU member has recovered and is back at headquarters. They have not
returned to Jewel 2. We have asked them to write down everything they
remembered before speaking with the rest of the team.

## Follow-up from Robert Hale

Robert Hale called us after the sighting report was circulated. He said
something had come back to him that he did not remember during our first
interview.

He remembers a machine in every room of Jewel on the Horizon. Each one had a
sensor attached, and the sensor had an unusual shape. Hale could not remember
what the machines were for. He said they were installed as part of the
original lighthouse, but were not included in the ordinary equipment list.

Jewel 2 was different. Hale remembers only one of these machines being
installed there. It was in the lowest level, in the room directly beneath the
heart room.

We checked this against the plans we have. That room is not identified as a
machine room, and no sensor is listed there. We asked Hale if he could draw
the sensor shape or describe the machine in more detail. He said he would
look through his old papers and call us back.

## open questions

- Was the lighthouse actually Jewel on the Horizon?
- Why was it visible beside the lake?
- Did it appear there at the same time as the north-shore sighting?
- Could either sighting be connected to the changes inside Jewel 2?
- What were the sensor machines for, and why was one placed beneath the heart room?

No answers yet. We are adding this report to the file and checking the
locations against the old coastal maps.
