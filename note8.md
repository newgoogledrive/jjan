# note 8 (jewel update 3, part 2)

**Jewel Updates — later the same day as Note 7**

This update has two separate parts. While RoTU continued following the lead
from Clause 17 at Jewel 2, JJAN spoke with Robert Hale, one of John Louken's
former assistants. RoTU's report came in later that day.

## Robert Hale

Hale is still alive. We found him through an old Harbor Lantern contact and
asked if he would speak with us. He agreed, but did not want to meet at the
company office. We spoke at his home and showed him the copy of Louken's will.

The will names Hale as a former assistant and leaves him Louken's drawing
instruments, measuring tools and architecture books. Hale confirmed that he
worked with Louken for several years. He helped with measurements for Jewel
on the Horizon and later gave advice during the Jewel 2 reconstruction.

Hale said Louken trusted him with ordinary plans, but kept some drawings
separate. When we asked about Clause 17 and the “record of its first room,”
Hale said he did not know what Louken meant by that phrase. He remembered a
separate set of survey papers that Louken was careful about letting anyone
handle. They were not stored with the usual Harbor Lantern drawings.

Hale thought the papers might have been moved to the coastal survey office.
He could not say when they were moved or who took them there. This may be
the same box listed in the old office catalogue and now missing, but we have
not confirmed that.

We told Hale that RoTU was checking the Clause 17 lead at Jewel 2. He asked
whether they planned to go below the connector room. When we said they did,
he told us not to let them open any door that was not on the plan.

We asked him why. He did not answer. He ended the interview.

Hale's account gives us a possible location for the separate survey papers,
but no explanation of what is in them. We passed his statement to NARAO and
started checking the coastal survey office records. At that point, RoTU had
not checked in.

## RoTU return

RoTU returned to headquarters later that day. Their check-in was late, but
all members were accounted for. NARAO has started recording their accounts
separately so the team can compare what each person remembers.

The team recovered a book from behind the metal cover marked with the Morse
code. It was wedged into the space behind the cover, rather than placed on a
shelf or stored with the plans. RoTU brought it back to headquarters. NARAO
is documenting its condition before anyone examines the contents. We do not
know yet if the book is connected to Clause 17.

## The first room

RoTU also reached the first room mentioned in Louken's will. None of the
members could clearly describe seeing a person, animal or other identifiable
thing inside. They all described the same sensation: something was watching
them.

The feeling continued after they left the room. Team members reported feeling
watched elsewhere in the lower levels, even when they were no longer near the
doorway. One described it as something standing just behind them. Turning
around did not make the feeling go away.

One member has quit RoTU. They said they could not believe what they had
experienced in the room. When asked what they meant, they did not give us a
clear description of what they saw. They spoke instead about the nightmares
that followed: the same room, the same feeling of being watched, and waking
up convinced that something was still there.

We will not pressure them to return. NARAO has offered to speak with them
separately if they choose. The remaining members are taking a break while
their accounts are recorded.

## What remains unclear

- The book's contents have not yet been examined.
- We do not know whether the book is the “record of its first room” from
  Clause 17.
- Hale could not explain the unmarked doors or the separate survey papers.
- Every RoTU member described the same watched feeling, but nobody could
  identify what caused it.
- One member has left the team after the experience and the nightmares.

No further entry is planned until NARAO has completed the first round of
interviews and documented the recovered book.
