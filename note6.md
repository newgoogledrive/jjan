# note 6 (jewel update 2)

**Jewel Updates — about a month after the first team went missing**

RoTU has set up a small headquarters in the old coast survey office. It is
close enough to coordinate the investigation, but not right next to Jewel 2.
We have a records room, a radio desk, and a place to review the plans without
having to carry everything back and forth.

We have made a logo for RoTU now. It will be used on the team's reports.

RoTU has started investigating the site. This time there are more precautions.
Nobody goes in alone, every visit has a check-in time, and another team stays
at the office monitoring the radio. We are keeping a clear route back out and
limiting how long anyone spends inside.

We are not treating this as an ordinary inspection. Three people disappeared
during the first reconstruction, and then the five-person team we sent went
missing. We are not going to repeat the same mistakes just to get a closer
look at a room.

## RoTU field report

The newest report is from the lower levels, at 05:00.

The team saw the heart in the heart room. It beat once. This is the only time
we have seen or heard it beat, and it matches the pulse reported in the
connector room.

The room changed while they were there. The measurements no longer matched
the plan, and the doorway they had used to enter was not where they expected
it to be. They followed the marked return route and got out. The team is safe.

They have not gone back in. We need to compare their notes and measurements
before deciding what to do next.

The old plans describe a machine in that room. The team says what they saw
did not look like a machine.

## next

RoTU is checking the radio recording and comparing the room measurements with
the Harbor Lantern plans. No further entry until that review is finished.
