# note 3

found in the Harbor Lantern files. not sure why it was left with the
reconstruction notes but it wasnt in the stuff they gave us last time.

The old lighthouse was called *Jewel on the Horizon*, or *Harbor Lantern
Jewel*. The one they started building again is usually called *Jewel 2*.
Sometimes its *Jewel Reconstruction* in the files.

We came here the first time after people working on Jewel 2 stopped showing up.
Harbor Lantern said the lower plans were restricted and the noise in the
connector room was just the building settling. we didnt have the plans to check.

This note says there is a conduit from the connector room down to the heart
room. It also says theres a pulse around 5 in the morning. No movement in the
Jewel 2 machinery when they checked it.

We have been digging through the rest of the records and talking to the people
who worked on the site. This is going to sound strange, but both Jewel on the
Horizon and Jewel 2 are alive. Not just making noises or moving because of bad
foundations. Alive.

Jewel 2 ate the three workers who disappeared in the lower levels. The notes
call it "fuel". We dont know what happened to them after that, and we are not
putting more detail in this note until we can confirm it.

The pulse in the connector room is coming from Jewel 2. It isnt a pump or a
piece of machinery. It is a heartbeat. The conduit goes down to the heart room
because thats where it is coming from.

Jewel on the Horizon is alive too. We still dont know where it went, or if it
can move, or if Harbor Lantern knew before they started building Jewel 2.
There are a lot of gaps in the records.

We are going out to the site in a week to investigate Jewel 2 ourselves. We
also need to talk to Harbor Lantern and find out what they knew about the
workers and the lower levels. "Interrogate" is probably the right word.

Hopefully they answer questions this time.
