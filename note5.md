# note 5 (jewel update 1)

**Jewel Updates — three days after Note 4**

JJAN and NARAO have agreed to combine the ongoing reports under one title:
**Jewel Updates**. Earlier files will remain available, but new findings will
be recorded as updates in this series.

We are also forming a small joint team for the next stage of the
investigation. The team will be called **RoTU**, short for **Rapid Operations
and Technical Unit**.

RoTU will review the site records, assess the equipment left at Jewel 2, and
prepare a plan for any further visit. It will include personnel from both
agencies. No one will return to Jewel 2 alone.

The five-person team sent to the site remains missing. Until we know more,
this should not be treated as a routine inspection.

## New report from the area

A shop owner from nearby has contacted NARAO after finding the lighthouse by
accident. They did not know about our missing team. They said there was blood
across the lantern glass and several shirts and other belongings left nearby.

The owner also said the lighthouse was not visible when they first passed the
area. On the way back, it was there. They are sure it was the same place.

The owner called NARAO. When they described the items, NARAO recognized them
as belonging to the five-person team and contacted JJAN. We have not yet
confirmed whether the belongings were left outside or brought from inside
Jewel 2. We also do not know what happened to the team. The owner's account
may explain how they found the site, but we have not been able to confirm the
appearance change ourselves.

The owner is safe and has been asked not to return to the site. NARAO and JJAN
are checking the report before deciding what to do next.

## Next steps

- NARAO is reviewing the plans and equipment list.
- JJAN is comparing the team's last message with the earlier field reports.
- Both agencies will verify the shop owner's report and coordinate any site work.

More information will be added when it has been confirmed.
