# note 7 (jewel update 3)

**Jewel Updates — one week after Note 6**

RoTU went back into the lower levels to repeat the measurements from the last
visit. They used the same tape, the same two people, and the same points on
the walls. Almost the whole heart room had changed again. The south wall was
farther away than the plan said, and the doorway was several metres from
where they had marked it last time.

One section had not changed. The space between the northwest corner and the
old conduit measured 8.10 by 6.20 metres, exactly as listed in the Harbor
Lantern plans. They measured it twice and got the same result both times.
The floor around it was damp, but that part of the room was dry.

The wall beside the matching section had a loose metal cover. There were
short and long marks cut into the surface behind it. The team photographed
the marks, copied them into the field book, and put the cover back. They did
not try to open the wall.

The marks look like Morse code. NARAO checked the spacing against the copy
from RoTU's field book. It reads:

`LOUKEN WILL / CLAUSE 17`

We checked the copy of John Louken's will in the public records. Clause 17
is only two lines, and it doesn't sound like the rest of the document:

`If the second light is ever made, the record of its first room is to remain
with the old survey papers. Do not give it to Harbor Lantern.`

The rest of the will is ordinary instructions about property and personal
effects. Clause 17 is handwritten in the margin and initialled J.L. There is
no date beside it. “The second light” probably means Jewel 2, but the will
doesn't explain what “the record of its first room” is.

We checked the public archive and the Harbor Lantern file copy. Neither has
the old survey papers attached. The catalogue lists a box of survey material
from the original lighthouse, but the box itself is missing. The entry was
changed by hand, and there is no name beside the change.

## Separate sighting

A NARAO member who is helping us with the archive work reported seeing a
lighthouse across the water one evening last week. This person is not part
of RoTU and was nowhere near the site visit.

They were on the north shore after sunset and saw a light beyond the water,
roughly opposite the old survey office. Through binoculars they could make
out the outline of a lighthouse. They said it looked like Jewel on the
Horizon, not Jewel 2. The light went out while they were watching. When they
looked again a few minutes later, there was nothing there.

The sighting has not been confirmed. We checked the charts for that stretch
of water and there is no lighthouse marked there. We also don't know if the
NARAO member saw the original lighthouse, a reflection, or something else.
Their written statement is being kept separate from the RoTU report.

RoTU left for the site again a few hours ago to check the location mentioned
in the will against the survey records. Their last radio check was made at
the upper entry before they went down. They have not checked in since. The
radio desk is still listening. Their return time has not passed yet, but we
are not sending anyone else after them.

For now, one part of the heart room stays the same, somebody hid a message
inside its wall, and Louken left a note about a record that nobody can find.
We also have a separate report of a lighthouse across the water.
