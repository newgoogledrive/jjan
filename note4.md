# note 4

**Jewel Updates — 2 weeks after the team left**

The five people we sent to look at Jewel 2 have been missing for two weeks.
This is the first update we've been able to agree on.

The plan was to check the outside first, then go inside with Harbor Lantern.
They sent one short message the first day. They had reached the tower and
were setting up their equipment. Nothing after that.

Their vehicle is still parked where they left it. Some of their equipment is
outside, but the rest is gone. We checked the access road and the nearby shore.
No one saw them leave. Harbor Lantern says it doesnt know where they are, and
has not let us back into the building.

We arent sending another team in alone. This is where NARAO comes in.

NARAO stands for **National Anomalous Research and Observation Agency**.
They investigate unusual events that have a physical location or evidence
that can be checked. They collect records, compare witness accounts, and
document changes at a site. They also have people trained to enter places
that might not be safe. They are not a police force, and they dont take over
missing-person cases. Their job here is to help us understand what Jewel 2
is doing and how to investigate it without losing more people.

JJAN has handled the older reports and the Harbor Lantern records. NARAO is
bringing a separate team to review the site and the equipment left behind.
For now, we are sharing copies of HL-2, the recovered Harbor Lantern plans,
and the notes from our first trip. They have asked us not to send anyone back
to the connector room until they have looked through the measurements.

We still dont know if Harbor Lantern is hiding something or if they really
dont understand what happened. They were supposed to be with our team when
they went inside. We need to speak to them again, with NARAO present.

## Harbor Lantern interview

We did get to speak with Harbor Lantern after all. We asked for the lead
architect, John Louken, but they confirmed that he died of old age four years
ago. There wont be an interview with him, and the company says his personal
work papers are incomplete.

One of the builders who worked under him spoke with us instead. He said
Louken was a very charming man, easy to talk to, and good at getting people
to trust him. But he was secretive about the lighthouse plans and never
explained why certain rooms had been added. That much matches what we already
found in the recovered files.

We told the builder that we had found the lower-level plans in Harbor
Lantern's file system. He went quiet for a while, then said, "I thought that
set was gone." He wouldnt say who had removed it or when. He only repeated
that Louken kept those drawings separate from the rest of the plans.

The interview didnt give us much that we didnt already know. But the builder
was surprised the lower plans still existed, which seems worth noting.

The five are still missing. We have no confirmation that they left the site.
We also have no confirmation that they are still inside.

## Next steps

- no solo visits to Jewel 2
