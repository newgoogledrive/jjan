# Last Will and Testament of John Louken

**Copy held in the JJAN archive**  
**Transcribed from the deposited copy. Original pagination retained where legible.**

I, John Louken, being of sound mind and acting freely, make this my last will
and testament. I revoke any earlier wills and amendments made by me.

## 1. Personal representative

I appoint Miriam Vale to act as executor of this will. If she is unable or
unwilling to act, I appoint Thomas Rell in her place.

## 2. Expenses

My executor shall pay my lawful debts, funeral expenses and the costs of
administering my estate as soon as reasonably possible.

## 3. Residence

I give my residence and its contents, except for items specifically named
elsewhere in this document, to my sister, Elian Louken.

## 4. Personal papers

My personal correspondence, journals and family records are to be given to
Elian Louken. She may dispose of any item she does not wish to keep.

## 5. Drafting instruments

My drawing instruments, measuring tools and books on architecture are to be
given to my former assistant, Robert Hale.

## 6. Harbor Lantern employment papers

Records relating to my ordinary employment, wages and company benefits may
be delivered to Harbor Lantern's records office.

## 7. Shares and payments

Any remaining shares, dividends or payment due to me from Harbor Lantern are
to be sold or collected by my executor and added to my estate.

## 8. Gifts

Small personal gifts not otherwise listed may be distributed by my executor
among my family and friends according to the accompanying memorandum.

## 9. Unlisted property

Any property not specifically disposed of in this will shall become part of
my residuary estate.

## 10. Residuary estate

I give the residue of my estate to Elian Louken, subject to the payment of
the expenses and gifts described above.

## 11. Plans and drawings

My general architectural drawings may be given to Robert Hale, except for
the sealed survey papers described in clause 16.

## 12. Copyright and attribution

Any plans or written work created by me may be identified as my work. This
clause does not grant permission to alter a plan in a way that changes its
recorded dimensions.

## 13. Unfinished work

My executor is not required to complete or deliver any unfinished private
design on my behalf.

## 14. Funeral arrangements

I request a private funeral. No company representatives are to speak on my
behalf or issue a statement in my name.

## 15. Interpretation

If any part of this will is found invalid, the remaining provisions shall
continue to have effect to the extent permitted by law.

## 16. Sealed survey papers

One sealed packet of survey papers is to remain with the old coastal survey
records. It is not part of my Harbor Lantern employment papers and is not to
be delivered with them.

## 17. Handwritten margin note

*If the second light is ever made, the record of its first room is to remain
with the old survey papers. Do not give it to Harbor Lantern.*

*Initialled J.L. No date recorded.*

## Execution

Signed by John Louken in the presence of the undersigned witnesses, who were
present at the same time and witnessed his signature.

**John Louken**  
Signature on deposited copy

**Witnesses:**  
Miriam Vale  
Thomas Rell
